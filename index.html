Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a Sundance® Spa cost?

Sundance® Spas range in price depending on the series and model. Contact your local dealer for current pricing and available promotions in your area.

How much does it cost to operate a hot tub?

The average monthly cost to operate a Sundance® Spa is approximately the same as your daily cup of coffee. Our spas are designed with energy-efficient features including full-foam insulation, tight-fitting covers, and efficient circulation pumps.

Actual costs vary based on your local utility rates, climate, and how often you use your spa. Many owners report monthly energy costs between $15 and $40. Your local Sundance® Spas dealer can provide more specific estimates for your area.

How long do Sundance® Spas last?

With proper maintenance, a Sundance® Spa can last 15 to 20 years or more. Our spas are built with high-quality materials and backed by comprehensive warranties for your peace of mind.

What are the health benefits of a hot tub?

Regular hot tub use can help reduce stress, relieve muscle tension, improve sleep quality, ease joint pain, and promote overall relaxation and well-being.

Where can I buy a Sundance® Spa?

Sundance® Spas are available exclusively through our authorized dealer network. Use our dealer locator to find a showroom near you where you can experience our spas in person.
